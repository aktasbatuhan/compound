## Limits

- FLORES sentences are news and encyclopedia prose. Chat, legal and code-mixed text may tokenize differently.
- Claude and Gemini are not measured yet; their tokenizers are not public and the paid count through OpenRouter was not run.
- Token cost is not quality. A model that needs fewer tokens can still give worse Turkish answers.
- Paid calls for this study: $0.00. OpenAI counts came from its free input-token endpoint.
